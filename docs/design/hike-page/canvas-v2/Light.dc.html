Advice vs data, 1 of 3

"Zion is busiest in summer, in August."

Partly true. Summer, yes, but June, not August. June 2023 was the busiest month: 564,036 visits, 12.20% of the 4,623,238 year total.

Source: NPS IRMA, Recreation Visits by Month
