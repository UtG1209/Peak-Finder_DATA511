Hike World

One 3D world for Angels Landing: the mountain with its trail and checkpoint flags, a ring of month pillars, and four cost towers. Hover anything for a frosted card, click to pin it and fly there. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom. Numbers are illustrative and typical (past years), not a quote.

3D view needs WebGL. The 2D charts on the page show the same numbers.
