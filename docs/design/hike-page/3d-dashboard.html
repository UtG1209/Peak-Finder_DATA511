3D dashboard: the trail, the twelve months and the trip cost in one scene

How to read it

Mountain: the trail, from the real elevation profile. The hiker stands where you are at the time on the slider.

Ring: twelve months. Height = typical high, glass band = your limit, figures = crowds, drops = wet days, haze = smoky days.

Cairns: typical trip cost per quarter: transport lodging food entry 15% buffer.

Sun: daylight of the selected month. Dashed arc: the trip from Seattle.

What if

Typical, from past data. Not a price quote.
