# Follow-ups: changes still needed (2026-11-24)

What this PR did, and what is left. Scope is 15 priority hikes (`pipeline/catalogue/priority_hikes.csv`); the final list is 15 to 20 and is not agreed yet.
Everything marked **Decision** needs the team's answer before work starts.

## Raised after looking at the running app

1. **Map pins are not visible when the app opens. Not a bug, but confusing. Decision.**
   - By design (CLAUDE.md section 2) the map is blurred until "Show hikes" is pressed, and the guide card sits over the middle of the map, so the blurred pins are mostly hidden behind it.
   - In `npm run dev` the map chunk is 1 MB unminified, so the map can take 10 s or more to appear; the production build is much faster.
   - Options: keep as is; move the guide card to a side so more pins show through the blur; or show the pins unblurred and keep only the card.
2. **Placeholder images.**
   - All 38 hikes already have a real, open-licence main photo with credit (23 CC BY-SA, 7 CC BY, 6 public domain, 2 CC0).
   - The placeholders are the **stop photos on the trail chart** (the "current stop" photo): 104 checkpoints on 22 hikes still point at generated `.svg` placeholders. These are all hikes outside the 15 priority hikes, whose profiles are still generated by `scripts/make_fake_data.py`. None of the 15 priority hikes has one (they show "No photo for this stop" or a real walk photo).
   - 35 stale `app/public/photos/*.svg` files exist only for those checkpoints.
   - Fix: either cut the data to the final hikes (the placeholders disappear with them) or source a Commons photo per stop. Real stop photos for the priority hikes are optional: 66 of their stops have none, which the page says in words.
3. **The "Sample data / placeholder numbers" notice was no longer true. Done in this PR.** The banner and the About notice now say it is work in progress: weather, crowds and air quality are real, and some hikes still use sample data for trailheads, travel, cost or profiles.
   - `meta.json` keeps `"fake": true` (it is one flag for the whole dataset). Flip it to `false`, and remove the notice, when every remaining hike is real. The simplest way is to cut the dataset to the final 15 to 20 hikes and fill the gaps below.

## Data gaps

- **Hikes outside the priority 15** (23 hikes): placeholder trailheads, end points, elevations, gain, closed months, generated profiles, and cost rows with placeholder entrance fees. They need the same treatment as the priority hikes or they get cut. **Decision.**
- **Fares:** 5 priority cost rows keep a placeholder fare (ATL to ATL for Alum Cave, four quarters, where nobody flies; Seattle to Bangor Q1). DOT Table 6 only lists busy markets.
- **Drive distance and time are understated** for Navajo Loop (about 1.3 mi), Hurricane Hill (about 17 mi) and Clingmans Dome (about 7 mi): ORS cannot reach the final road, so `pipeline/catalogue/drive_points.csv` routes to the nearest reachable point. Add a sourced extra distance, or accept and say so on About.
- **Glacier closed months:** NPS says only "closed for the season", no typical dates, so none are recorded (Highline, Grinnell).
- **Weak figures:**
  - Fairy Falls gain is a net 54 ft (NPS gives none).
  - Alum Cave gain is net.
  - Glacier Highline max elevation (7,273 ft) is route-derived.
  - Hurricane Hill and Wheeler Peak summit elevations have no NPS figure and stay placeholders.
- **Fees:**
  - Delicate Arch (timed entry) and Glacier Highline (vehicle reservation) permit fees are UNVERIFIED and left at $0.
  - Great Smoky Mountains entrance is $0 UNVERIFIED (parking tag price not read).
  - Permit extras charged only when awarded (Angels Landing $3 per person, Half Dome $10) are not modelled.
- **Profiles:** Cadillac North Ridge (scale 1.30) and Glacier Highline (scale 1.21) use a documented scale because the OSM route is far from the NPS length. Fairy Falls and Grinnell still start at the old walk start, not the new trailheads.
- **Advice cards:** 7 real cards; the Las Vegas fare card is hard-coded in `pipeline/advice.py`, not computed.

## Code and tests

- Labels "Start" and "Back" overlap under the daylight bar of the trail chart on short hikes (cosmetic).
- The e2e test "with reduced motion the bubbles jump straight to the new size" is timing-sensitive: it passes alone and sometimes fails in the full run.
- The cloud transition's reverse plays on browser Back and when the walk's own Back button returns; an unused saved scroll entry (2 h) can replay it once if a hike is reopened without a walk. Minor.
- CI `pipeline` job: weather tests use committed fixtures (`pipeline/checks/fixtures/ghcn/`, Springdale only) so NOAA outages no longer fail CI. EPA and NPS tests still download their raw files on a cold cache.
- `pipeline/build.py` now takes closed months from `pipeline/catalogue/closed_months.csv` and copies `advice.json`. `pipeline/cost.py --only <csv>` runs the full GSA and EIA cost build for a list of hikes and keeps every other hike's rows. `pipeline/meta.py` merges `data/processed/lane2/cost_sources.json`.
